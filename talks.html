BayScen: Standards-Grounded Scenario Generation for Autonomous Vehicle Testing

ISSRE 2026 · Research Track · Limassol, Cyprus · October 2026

Conference presentation of BayScen: how an ISO 34503-grounded capability abstraction makes combinatorial coverage of the operational design domain tractable, while a Bayesian network learned from real-world data keeps the generated scenarios physically plausible.

PWCC: Probability-Weighted Combinatorial Coverage for Risk-Informed Scenario Testing of Autonomous Systems

ASE 2026 · Research Track · Munich, Germany · October 2026

Main-conference talk on turning coverage from a descriptive statistic into a stopping decision you can defend: weight each combination of operating conditions by how likely it is on the road, and stop testing once the untested share of expected operation falls below a risk-calibrated threshold.
